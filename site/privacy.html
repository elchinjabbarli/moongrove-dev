Moongrove — Privacy

Gizlilik / Privacy. Moongrove collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics and no trackers in the game.

What stays on your device

Level progress, stars, dew, boosters and settings are stored locally in your browser (localStorage) or in the app's own storage. Clearing the app data or site data removes them. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Ads & purchases

In the iOS app, ads (if shown) are served by Apple-authorized ad networks and purchases are processed entirely by Apple App Store. Any ad personalization choice you make is stored only on your device. The web version at this address contains no ads and no purchases.
